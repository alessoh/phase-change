## Q1. Flagship documented deployments and their reported savings

### Takeaway
The best-documented, quantified successes are parcel and truck routing (UPS ORION at $300–400M/yr; Walmart at 30M miles avoided), network and fulfillment design (Amazon's regionalization, with >$0.45/unit US savings), on-demand dispatch (Meituan with about $0.23B/yr; Lyft with >$30M/yr) and end-to-end planning/scheduling (Intel with $25.4B cumulative since 2009). The Franz Edelman Award is the most reliable source of vetted numbers. From 2023 to 2026 it has leaned heavily toward logistics: Walmart won in 2023, Amazon, WM, Flipkart and Lufthansa were 2025 finalists, and Microsoft's cloud supply chain won in 2026.

### Cited Findings

**UPS (ORION and successors)**
- ORION (On-Road Integrated Optimization and Navigation) won the 2016 Franz Edelman Award — [INFORMS UPS 2016 Edelman](https://www.informs.org/Recognizing-Excellence/Award-Recipients/UPS-2016-Edelman); [GlobeNewswire, Apr 12 2016](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2016/04/12/828097/0/en/UPS-Wins-2016-INFORMS-Franz-Edelman-Award-for-Changing-the-Future-of-Package-Delivery.html)
- ORION cost $250M to build and deploy. It was expected to save $300–400M per year and to cut CO2 by about 100,000 t per year — [Analytics Magazine](http://analytics-magazine.org/ups-wins-2016-edelman-award-for-or-achievement/); [ORMS Today 2016](https://pubsonline.informs.org/do/10.1287/orms.2016.03.10/full/)
- Reported impact: about 100 million miles and 10 million gallons of fuel saved per year. Routes averaged 6–8 fewer miles per driver per day, and the system evaluates more than 200,000 alternative ways to run one route — [BSR case study](https://www.bsr.org/en/case-studies/center-for-technology-and-sustainability-orion-technology-ups); [Environment+Energy Leader](https://www.environmentenergyleader.com/stories/ups-routing-system-to-save-10m-gallons-of-fuel-annually,8464)

This disagrees with the $250M build cost in the Edelman-era materials. The $1B figure probably counts the wider telematics and data programme. Treat it as unverified.
- Network Planning Tools (NPT) optimize package flow through hubs and sorts. UPS targeted full deployment by 2020 and $100–200M in savings and cost avoidance — [UPS Sustainability, Optimized Network](https://sustainability.ups.com/committed-to-more/optimized-network/); [Joachim Weise on NPT](https://joachimweise.github.io/post/2019-11-23-ups-npt/). **Hospital scheduling**
- The evidence I found is mostly academic. ### Inferences
- The largest verified dollar figures come from long-lived, network-scale programmes (Intel, UPS, Amazon), not from single algorithms. Payback came from repeated daily decisions at huge volume: small per-route or per-unit gains multiplied by billions of events.
- Since 2023 the logistics winners and finalists increasingly pair ML prediction with MIP or heuristic optimization (DoorDash, Meituan, Microsoft IFS, Lyft RL). Pure OR models are giving way to predict-then-optimize pipelines.
- Walmart selling its internal router is a notable pattern: in-house optimization is becoming a commercial product. Amazon has a partial parallel in AWS supply chain services.

## Q3. What made deployments succeed

### Takeaway
The recurring success factors in the documented cases are long, phased rollouts with pilots; designing for frontline adoption; big investment in data and ML prediction layers ahead of the optimizer; decomposing problems into tractable pieces; and executive sponsorship tied to measurable KPIs.

### Cited Findings
- UPS rolled ORION out in phases over years (research, then pilots, then national rollout, then continuous improvement), and BSR highlights driver adoption and change management as central — [BSR ORION adoption case study](https://www.bsr.org/en/case-studies/center-for-technology-and-sustainability-orion-technology-ups); [MetaLumna timeline (secondary)](https://metalumna.com/articles/ups-orion-billion-dollar-ai-routing)
- Amazon's regionalization ran a pilot before full deployment by March 2023, over about 1.5 years of OR work combining region design, network optimization, inventory-speed tradeoffs, software changes and operational changes — [IJAA Amazon paper](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/inte.2025.0295)
- DoorDash and Meituan built ML prediction layers first (ready time, travel time, acceptance), then an optimizer, and partitioned markets into zones so the problem stays tractable — [DoorDash engineering](https://doordash.engineering/2020/02/28/next-generation-optimization-for-dasher-dispatch-at-doordash/); [Meituan IJAA](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/inte.2023.0084)
- Intel used problem decomposition with MIP to make enterprise-wide planning fast enough to evaluate many more business scenarios — [INFORMS Intel release](https://www.informs.org/News-Room/INFORMS-Releases/Awards-Releases/Operations-Research-Used-to-Maximize-Revenue-and-Minimize-Cost-in-Corporate-Decision-Making-Intel-Awarded-the-2020-INFORMS-Edelman-Award)
- Lufthansa's disruption tool started at one airline (SWISS) before scaling across the group, and WM's success is credited to a cross-team collaboration — [Resoundingly Human: Lufthansa](https://resoundinglyhuman.com/episodes/2025-franz-edelman-award-finalist-luftansa/); [Resoundingly Human: WM](https://resoundinglyhuman.com/episodes/2025-franz-edelman-award-finalist-wm/)
- Counterexample: PSR at US railroads (schedule-driven operations) cut costs, but critics find near-zero service gains. Optimization aimed at the operating ratio can improve cost without improving customer outcomes — [Railway Age](https://www.railwayage.com/news/clear-evidence-near-zero-psr-service-improvements/)

### Inferences
- Incremental rollout (one site, airline or region at a time) appears in almost every success story. It lets teams calibrate models against the tacit knowledge of drivers and dispatchers and build trust before scaling.
- Success metrics are multi-dimensional (cost, speed, CO2, service). Deployments that report several KPIs, like UPS, Walmart and Molslinjen, seem to win organizational support more easily.

### Gaps
- Few sources give failure rates or failed deployments. Documented cases suffer from survivorship bias, because Edelman submissions and vendor case studies select for successes.
- I found no quantitative evidence on the relative weight of data quality versus algorithm quality.
