## Q1. Upcoming event: dates, format, eligibility, team size, prizes, sponsors, partners, judging, track hints

### Takeaway
The event behind luma.com/z3za7zow is the **Hack-Nation 7th Global AI Hackathon on October 3-4, 2026**. ## Q3. Past winners, what winning projects looked like, and what judges reward

### Takeaway
Winners ship a **working, deployed full-stack product within about 18-24 hours**. They pair a real technical core (graph algorithms, trained ML models with reported metrics, multi-agent protocols) with a clear business or impact story and a live demo. Rubrics and descriptions stress technical execution, innovation, impact and venture potential.

### Cited Findings
- **1st prize, OpenAI track (2026):**
  - The project built a communication graph from corporate email and ran graph-theoretic analysis on it, "automating what costs six figures in consulting".
  - It had a fully deployed backend and frontend, built in about 18 hours.
  - Prize: EUR 7.5k cash plus a trip to San Francisco. The event claimed 3000+ participants.
  - Source: [LinkedIn post snippet, Karlo Vrancic](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/karlovrancic_we-just-won-the-first-prize-at-hack-nations-activity-7426634987640242176-a9PX)
- **NexusFlow (Feb 2026, winner of the VC Big Bet Supply Chain Agents challenge):**
  - It is an "Internet of Agents" orchestration layer. Agents discover partners through a NANDA registry (semantic search over UN/LOCODE and ISO capability tags), negotiate with MCP/JSON-RPC 2.0 messages (supply.procure, supply.offer, logistics.book), and self-heal by rerouting around failures such as port strikes.
  - It has a React graph-visualization dashboard, and the pitch frames the market as "$115 trillion global supply chain".
  - Source: [GitHub samgbm/nexusflow](https://github.com/samgbm/nexusflow)
- **BoneBot (Jul 2026, London hub winner, Challenge 05):**
  - Model: an NHANES-trained ridge regression on 1,119 subjects, with reported metrics (triage AUC 0.867, full model AUC 0.789 vs. baseline 0.772, MAE 0.727). The "model predicts, LLM only explains".
  - Live Vercel demo, built by a 4-person team in 24 hours.
  - Source: [GitHub eyavuz21/bonebot](https://github.com/eyavuz21/bonebot)
- **Market Link Network (Nov 2025):**
  - Placement: "Top 4 Team Overall", "Challenge Group Winner", "Creativity Prize Winner".
  - Stack: OpenAI embeddings with kNN, Gemini reasoning, FastAPI/Next.js, D3 network visualization, and expected-value arbitrage calculations.
  - Source: [GitHub Cyro292/hacknation2025](https://github.com/Cyro292/hacknation2025)
- The Databricks challenge rubric weighted technical accuracy 35%, innovation 30%, social impact 25%, UX 10% — [GitHub neo-command](https://github.com/sharckhai/neo-command)
- The Venture Track selects the "Top 15 teams ... based on technical execution and venture potential" — [hack-nation.ai/venture-track snippet](https://hack-nation.ai/venture-track)
- Results are presented in live "Final Pitches" sessions over Zoom, for example — [Luma l13msv1r](https://luma.com/l13msv1r); [Luma b69eqqqu](https://luma.com/b69eqqqu)
- A winners showcase exists but could not be fetched — [projects.hack-nation.ai/winners](https://projects.hack-nation.ai/winners)

### Inferences
- Judges seem to reward, in order:
  1. A working, deployed demo
  2. Measurable technical rigor, such as metrics against a baseline
  3. A quantified business or impact pitch, such as a dollar market size or replaced consulting cost
  4. A good fit with the sponsor's challenge
- For an entry where autonomous research agents improve a logistics algorithm, this suggests showing a **before/after benchmark**, for example route cost or on-time rate against a baseline heuristic. It also suggests showing a live agent loop and giving a clear venture framing.

### Gaps
- I found no official winners list per edition. The showcase page and the LinkedIn announcements were blocked.
- I could not confirm the prize tier structure (1st/2nd/3rd per challenge vs. overall).
